# QST-SYNTH-0001: Agent-Authored Thermal Stress Worlds

Status: Active — R0/R1 accepted; R2 generation stopped at useful-delivery scientific-decision boundary
Priority: P1
Authority: M's September action authorization in Issue #79; original campaign Issue #30
Research scope: thermal-storage

## Question

Can bounded agent-authored synthetic eclipse/degradation/thermal-buffer/sensor-failure worlds improve an SNS policy's survival on independently seeded holdouts without unacceptable loss of useful host-energy delivery?

The already-imported synthetic_worlds instrument is model-evidence infrastructure. No new import or private dependency is needed. This quest does not change protected physical assumptions, select a hardware architecture, train a model or establish flight readiness.

## First executable acceptance slice — R0

Prepare one reviewed, machine-readable tiny-campaign protocol and a non-generating validation harness. Inventory the actual generator and SNS thermal/eclipsing evaluator interfaces. Identify any missing adapter as explicit implementation work. Freeze:

- three arms: fixed handwritten scenarios; random bounded scenarios; agent-proposed bounded curriculum;
- pinned generator/SNS source identities and public import manifest;
- development and independently seeded holdout sets, with no overlap or post-result replacement;
- policy family, matched world/record/runtime/storage budgets;
- model-supported ranges, anomaly types, exclusions, all metric definitions and failure accounting;
- survival and delivery comparison criteria, including an explicit tolerance justified before results;
- a tiny deterministic reproduction command and falsifiers.

R0 may author code/tests/configuration and qualify the protocol; it must not generate campaign results before the protocol is accepted. No field is silently filled with favorable arbitrary values. If the evaluator lacks a necessary metric or physical assumption, record the missing interface and propose a bounded adapter; reserve genuine scientific choices for Monthly/human.

## Staged campaign

R1: implement/qualify the minimal adapter and tiny development-only fixture under the accepted protocol. Target at most three development worlds per arm initially, with a separate untouched holdout allocation; exact counts are frozen by R0 inside standing ceilings.

R2: execute the frozen matched comparison once, including independently seeded holdouts, retaining invalid, failed, excluded and budget-rejected worlds. Compare node survival, depletion, thermal-bound violations, useful delivery, curtailment, heater demand, PCM contribution/mass and coverage where the accepted model supports them. Unsupported quantities are declared missing, never synthesized as physical evidence.

R3: Weekly reviews reproducibility and criteria. Close with supported benefit, no demonstrated benefit, or falsification on the declared model/scope. Open a distinct bounded successor only when the evidence earns it. A protocol or adapter alone does not close this research quest.

## Falsifiers and limits

Reject the curriculum hypothesis if benefit disappears on independent holdouts, survival comes from trivial permanent idle/shutdown, useful delivery breaches the frozen tolerance, or recipe coverage omits declared risks. A changed evaluator/holdout after freeze invalidates the comparison. Preserve every unfavorable result. Synthetic outcomes concern declared models only.

## September 20 Weekly recovery disposition

R1 is accepted as a qualified, non-generating interface result. Its refusal to invent `useful_host_delivery_Wh` for electrical-failure cases is now an explicit campaign boundary rather than an adapter TODO.

The accepted thermal evaluator integrates the full requested base/heater loads through the eclipse and reports total consumed energy, derated available energy, final electrical margin, and PASS/FAIL. It does not model depletion time, load shedding, partial host service, or a cutoff trajectory. Therefore a numeric partial-delivery value on electrical failure is not recoverable from current accepted semantics without adding a new service/depletion model.

**R2 status: `NEEDS_SCIENTIFIC_DECISION`.**

Do not:

- map electrical failure to zero useful delivery merely to unblock the campaign;
- derive a favorable partial-delivery value from final negative margin;
- drop failed worlds from accounting;
- touch the sealed holdout while this boundary is unresolved.

The next lawful SYNTH action is a separate scientific decision: either authorize a bounded evaluator extension that models partial service/depletion, or explicitly revise the frozen useful-delivery comparison requirement. Until then, other independent quests remain executable.

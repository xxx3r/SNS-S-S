# QST-STOR-0001: SNS Storage Geometry Audit

Status: Completed  
Updated: 2026-07-10  
Tags: [STOR, POWER, SIM, TEST, DOCS]

## Hypothesis

If SNS seed storage is derived from spherical core volume, battery allocation, and volumetric energy density, then the conceptual `0.1–10 Wh` range can be replaced with a defensible capacity envelope and explicit survival limits.

## Method

- Inputs: 10/20/30 mm cores; 15/30/50% battery fractions; 220/450/600/1000 Wh/L; sleep and active load sweeps; 0.5/2/12/72 h shadows.
- Procedure: Evaluate the full Cartesian sweep under declared efficiency, reserve, duty-cycle, density, PV-power, and C-rate assumptions.
- Metrics: usable battery energy, qualified survival duration, required storage, storage margin, ideal PV fill time, charge-limited fill time, battery mass estimate, PASS/FAIL.
- Stress test: Re-run the sweep at 0%, 1%, 5%, 10%, and 100% active duty during shadow.

## Result

- 1,240 / 1,296 baseline scenarios PASS the geometry gate.
- 10 mm usable capacity spans 0.0138–0.2094 Wh, not 0.1–10 Wh.
- 55 of 56 failures occur in 10 mm cores; 49 occur at 72 h shadow.
- Baseline overall pass rate falls from 95.7% at 1% shadow-active duty to 68.2% at continuous activity.
- Ideal PV fill time is misleadingly short; a 1C acceptance limit sets a 48-minute minimum to reach the modeled 80% usable state.

## Risks

- PASS excludes thermal derating, heater demand, radiation, aging, cell packaging overhead, wiring, converter losses beyond the declared efficiency, and deployment constraints.
- Battery bulk density is a configurable mass-estimation assumption, not a chemistry-specific cell mass model.
- The 1% active duty baseline is an operational policy hypothesis and must be mission-tested.

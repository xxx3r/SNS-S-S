# Summer 2026 Constraint Map

## Physics-constrained

- diffraction and beam divergence
- receiver coupling and pointing error
- radiative thermal rejection
- orbital and line-of-sight geometry
- collection area limits at small scale

## Engineering-constrained

- flexible-PV lifetime under combined stressors
- deployment mechanism mass and failure probability
- survival storage and pulse power
- radiation-tolerant low-power compute
- tunable metasurface integration
- manufacturing yield and quality control
- swarm coordination under scarcity and intermittent links

## Ecosystem-constrained

- limited hardware evidence
- need for external validators and collaborators
- need for an inspectable open framework
- unclear near-term customer budgets
- risk of translating long-horizon architecture into near-term overclaims

## Dominant Summer 2026 bottleneck

The dominant bottleneck is no longer imagination. It is whether the model is disciplined enough that an outside expert can identify wrong assumptions, reproduce outputs, and propose a better measurement.

These paths do not require eight concurrent quests. A quest can serve more than one path.

**[Formula]** math/theory/explicit assumptions/optional Lean proofs. **[Mechanism]** simulation or separately identified physical bench measurements. **[Implementation]** integrated prototype then space qualification. Distinguish formal consistency, measurement integrity and engineering closure. Never promote synthetic evidence to hardware readiness.

**External confidence**: dated primary literature, adverse data, applicability to a specific claim, environment, link, ⬆️/⬇️/↔️. Weekly is the independent telescope. **Internal confidence**: accepted exact-source theoretical checks, simulations, bench or physical evidence, limits, falsifier and direction. Daily wheel is the engine. UNKNOWN is valid for either; no invented weighted average or automatic Monthly belief edit.

**Research wheel:** Yesterday Post-Game → Pre-Game → Daily → Post-Game → tomorrow Pre-Game. Share a scientific *question*, not last agent's authority. Each fresh agent rereads accepted main and live ownership. Any unowned graph-ready **active** quest may be chosen within standing scope; no extra AUTH hop. Pre-Game may perform bounded zero-world docs/tests prep; Daily qualifies a test; Post-Game challenges claim/metric meaning or advances another independent test. No duplicate owner, repeated consumed world, or new handoff ledger. Existing receipts/tests/CI still apply.

**Scientific handoff in existing output:** path(s), achieved [Formula]/[Mechanism]/[Implementation] level; intended claim; actual observable/result; source and evidence quality; external/internal confidence arrow and UNKNOWN; exact limitations; one smallest discriminating next test and independent fallback. The 0.1–1% daily progress goal is directional, not measurable without a defensible denominator.

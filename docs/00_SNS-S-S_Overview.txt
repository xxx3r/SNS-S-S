SNS-S-S :: Summer 2026 Overview
================================

Purpose
-------
SNS-S-S is a Python simulation and research-calendar framework for:

1. SNS swarm dynamics.
2. Electrical and thermal energy-chain accounting.
3. Asteroid and GEO mission environments.
4. Asteroid Resource Confidence Index (ARCI) research.
5. Literature-to-belief-to-quest execution.

Current phase
-------------
The project is not hardware-ready. It is ready to become a formal research
instrument. Summer 2026 therefore prioritizes legibility, reproducibility,
uncertainty, and disciplined external critique.

Strategic interpretation
------------------------
The long-horizon Solar-Nano-Sphere remains an intelligent energy mote operating
inside future SBSP infrastructure. The strongest early product frame is
resource and environmental intelligence:

    SNS does not mine asteroids.
    SNS helps decide which asteroids deserve miners.

Canonical scenarios
-------------------
- Asteroid resource-intelligence swarm.
- GEO / SBSP field-diagnostics swarm.

Canonical metrics
-----------------
- harvested, stored, delivered, and curtailed energy
- node survival and health
- coverage
- role and mode distributions
- temperature proxies
- ARCI score, confidence, and uncertainty

Design constraint
-----------------
The 10 mm seed is a survival-and-control node, not a warehouse for the full
output of a large deployable PV kite. Storage, beaming, and thermal control must
therefore be modeled as network architecture rather than one scalar battery.
